# 0001 — Record decisions and keep a devlog

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context
This is a portfolio project with a big vision and a small target. Two risks:
scope creep (the vision is always more interesting than the next step), and
losing the *why* behind choices, which is exactly what a reviewer or interviewer
asks about.

## Decision
- Choices go into numbered decision records in `docs/decisions/`.
- Inspirations go into `docs/INSPIRATIONS.md`, each with a concrete "take".
- Every work session ends with a dated entry in `docs/DEVLOG.md`.
- New ideas go to the parking lot in `docs/SCOPE.md`; pulling one into scope needs a decision record.

## Consequences
A little writing per session. In return the devlog doubles as blog/LinkedIn
material and the decision records answer "why did you do it this way?".

# 0020 — Adam: the engine that runs people

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-14

## Context
People are run by several mechanisms decided separately: being woken by the
event queue (ADR-0007), walking, seeing and knowledge (ADR-0015), weighted goals
(ADR-0016). They need one owner, the way Molly owns the world-level jobs.

## Decision
**Adam** is the engine that runs people. For each person it:
- wakes them on their events (arrival, sight, threshold, Molly's signals),
- re-scores their goals and picks an action (ADR-0016),
- plans walks and books arrival and sight events (ADR-0015),
- records what they learn as `knows` links (ADR-0015, 0019).

Later, Adam also **gives people their character**: assigns trait nodes (brave,
stubborn, curious) that scale goal weights and determination.

Adam does not decide *what* people can do; actions, needs and goals come from
packs (ADR-0018). Adam is the machinery, packs are the content.

# 0024 — Adam in M3: rules in packs, verbs in the engine

- Status: accepted (technical; designed by Claude during M3, open to change)
- Date: 2026-11-14

## Context
M3 makes people live: walk, see, explore, remember, rest at night. ADRs 0007,
0015, 0016 and 0020 set the principles; this records the concrete design.

## Decision

### Packs say why, the engine says how
- Pack format v0.2 adds mind rules:
  - **need**: `start`, `rate` (per day), `urgentAt`. Each person's level is a
    lazy value on their `has` edge.
  - **goal**: `tier` (1–4), `weight`.
  - **action**: `do` (an engine verb) and `serves` (how much it helps each goal).
  - entities can be `memorable`: people form sightings of them.
- **Verbs** are what Adam can physically do: `explore`, `wander`, `rest` for now.
  New verbs (gather, build) are engine work; new reasons to use them are pack work.
- Mind expressions can read `needs.X`, `night`, `knows.X` (how many of a
  memorable type the person knows), `self.attr` and `doing`. Archie checks that
  every `needs.X` / `knows.X` and every goal named in `serves` exists.

### Deciding (ADR-0016 in numbers)
- Score of an action = Σ goal weight × contribution.
- A tier-1 goal is urgent above `0.6 + determination × commitment × 0.3`; then
  only actions that help an urgent goal are considered.
- The current action gets `determination × commitment × 0.25`.
- Commitment = 0.5 + 0.5 × progress through the current walking leg.
- People re-decide on: arriving, Molly's signals (dusk, dawn), a need crossing
  `urgentAt`, their personal `urgentAt + determination × 0.3`, or 0.05, and at
  most every 0.25 days.

### Walking and seeing (ADR-0015 in practice)
- A* on the cell grid (water > 0.5 blocks, slope costs extra), then straightened.
- One `activity` event per leg carries the whole path and arrival time; position
  in between is computed, never stored. Interruptions emit `node.moved` at the
  computed point.
- When a leg starts, every memorable thing that will come within sight radius
  (6 cells by day, 2 at night) gets an `adam.see` event at that exact moment.
- Explore picks among 12 directions at a Lévy-like distance (6–40 cells), scored
  by novelty (far from what they know), fertility and heading; wander picks a
  short nearby leg.

### Knowledge
- A sighting claim is a `Sighting` node with `x, y`, linked to its subject by an
  `about` edge. One claim per thing-at-a-place; each person links to it with
  `knows { learnedAt, confidence }`. Seeing it again after 5 days refreshes
  `learnedAt`.

### Molly in M3
- Only day/night: `signal` events at dusk (21:00) and dawn (06:00), which wake
  everyone. The day starts at dawn: time fraction 0 = 06:00, night from 0.625.

### Engine state vs world truth
- Adam keeps runtime caches (scheduled tickets, known counts, spatial index,
  last choice scores) that can be rebuilt from the graph. Everything that
  matters to the story goes through events.

## Consequences
- Five simulated days with six people take about 0.2 s; the log grows by roughly
  600 events per day for six people, dominated by movement.
- With dense trees, people learn their surroundings within a day and then settle
  into local wandering; M4 gives them a reason to move again (gathering wood).
- `pick()` was added to the language for names.

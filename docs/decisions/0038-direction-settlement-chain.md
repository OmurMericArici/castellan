# 0038 — Direction: the settlement chain comes first

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24

## Context
The goal of Castellan has always been the bonds that build settlements: houses, villages,
towns, cities, castles and the trade between them (VISION, the long road). While packs were
added in M5 and M5b, the work drifted into depth of the mind instead: `hostility`,
`psychopathy`, murder and witnesses, and a parking lot whose items are now mostly social
psychology (trust, grief, shame, contagion, gossip). Nothing chose this; it happened one
pack at a time. The castle, the original idea, sits as one line at the end of the list.

## Options
- A — Accept the drift and redefine the project as a model of human psychology and society.
  Honest to what was built, but not what the project is for, and it claims a realism the
  hand-written rules can't back.
- B — Keep the definition and steer back: a direction rule for every new pack, and a parking
  lot split so mind ideas stop competing with chain links.

## Decision
B. Every new pack or primitive must add a link to the settlement chain (house → village →
town → city → castle, plus trade) or a bond between its links. Anything else goes to
"Mind (not now)" in the parking lot.

## Consequences
- The mind built in M5b stays; it is the motive force the chain needs (wanting, remembering,
  telling). It is deep enough for now.
- After v0.1 the next work comes from the "Chain" list: village ties, paths and roads,
  skill and specialisation, ownership and reciprocity, trade routes, then castles.
- ADR-0035 (the engine offers every tool to model behaviour) still holds as an engine
  principle; this record only decides the order in which content is written.
- Mind ideas are kept, not deleted. One comes forward only when a chain link needs it
  (for example, trust may be needed for trade).

## Discussion
- 2026-11-24 — Asked whether the project had turned from a castle/city builder into a model
  of human psychology. The user: the definition does not move; the psychology depth came
  unintentionally while adding packs, and the real aim was always the links between house,
  town, city, castle and trade. Decided to keep the definition, add a direction rule and
  split the parking lot.
